THRIVELENS 300K DATASET — NO-LOGIN PC BUILDER

1. Extract this ZIP file.
2. Double-click RUN_THRIVELENS_300K_BUILDER.cmd.
3. Leave the window open until it reports READY.

No account or sign-in is required.
No split files need to be downloaded or combined manually.

The final files are saved automatically to:
C:\Users\thaba\OneDrive\Documents\ThriveLens 2026\Training and Validation Model

Final dataset filename:
ThriveLens_OpenFood_FoodR1_Kaggle_300K.zip

The launcher downloads from the public official Fruits-360, Open Images, and
Nutrition5k sources, builds the Kaggle archive locally, validates the ZIP and
dataset splits, and places the Food-R1 notebook beside it.

Keep the computer connected to power and the internet while it runs.
At least 15 GB of free disk space and Python 3.11 or newer are required.
